These terms apply to text messages sent by Safety Button, a personal emergency alert device. By agreeing to receive messages from a Safety Button device, you accept the terms below.

Program description

Safety Button sends emergency alert messages when the device owner presses the button on their device. Messages include the device's location and are followed by periodic location updates during the alert period. The program also sends occasional device status messages, such as a low battery notice. This is not a marketing program and no promotional messages are sent.

Who receives messages

Messages go only to phone numbers the device owner has added to their device. Each recipient consents before their number is added. There is no public sign-up.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on device use. Most devices send one status message per day or fewer. When the button is pressed, recipients receive an alert message followed by location updates approximately once per minute for up to 30 minutes.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Safety Button does not charge for messages; charges from your mobile carrier are your responsibility.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages after that. You may also ask the device owner to remove your number from the device at any time.

Getting help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or email kiran.home@gmail.com.

Not an emergency service

Safety Button is a personal alert device, not a monitored emergency service and not a substitute for calling emergency services. It does not contact police, fire, or medical responders. In an emergency, call 911.
