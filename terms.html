Messaging Terms & Conditions

Safety Button Alerts · Operated by Kiran Akkineni · Last updated 26 July 2026

These terms govern the Safety Button Alerts text messaging program. By consenting to receive messages from a Safety Button device, you agree to these terms.

Program description

Safety Button Alerts sends emergency alert messages on behalf of the owner of a Safety Button device. When the device's button is pressed, designated recipients receive a text message containing the device's location, followed by location update messages approximately once per minute for up to 30 minutes. The program also sends occasional device status messages, such as a low battery notice or a warning that a device has stopped checking in.

No marketing, no third-party messaging

This program sends transactional emergency alerts only. It does not send marketing, promotional, or advertising messages of any kind. It is not used to send messages on behalf of any third party, and it is not used for lead generation, affiliate marketing, or any shared or resold messaging arrangement. Every message originates from a device owned and configured by the recipient's own family.

How you join

You are added to this program only by the owner of a Safety Button device, and only after you have given consent in person or by phone. There is no public sign-up form and no keyword sign-up. Your number is never purchased, rented, or obtained from a third party. A full description of the consent process is on our home page.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on device use. In normal operation a device sends one status message per day or fewer. When the button is pressed, you will receive one alert message followed by location updates approximately once per minute for up to 30 minutes.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Safety Button does not charge for messages; any charges from your mobile carrier are your responsibility.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages after that. You may also ask the device owner to remove your number from the device at any time, which takes effect immediately.

Getting help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or email kiran.home@gmail.com.

Carriers and delivery

Supported carriers include AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon Wireless, U.S. Cellular, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery depends on cellular network availability and cannot be guaranteed.

Not an emergency service

Safety Button is a personal alert device, not a monitored emergency service and not a substitute for emergency services. It does not contact police, fire, or medical responders, and no one monitors alerts on your behalf. In an emergency, call 911.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for how information is handled. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
