Privacy Policy

Safety Button · Last updated 25 July 2026

Safety Button is a personal emergency alert device. When the button is pressed, the device sends a text message and places an automated voice call to the phone numbers configured by the device owner. This policy explains what information the service handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Phone numbers of the recipients the device owner chooses to alert.
  • Device location (GPS coordinates, or an approximate location derived from nearby cell towers) captured at the moment the button is pressed and during the alert period that follows.
  • Device status, such as battery level and periodic check-ins used to confirm the device is working.

How we use it

Information is used for one purpose: delivering alerts from the device to the recipients the owner has designated. Location is included in those alerts so recipients can find the person carrying the device.

Mobile numbers are never shared

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Numbers are used only to deliver alerts from the owner's own device, and are disclosed only to the telecommunications provider that transmits those messages.

Consent

Recipients are added by the device owner. Each recipient gives consent before their number is added, and the owner can remove any number at any time. There is no public sign-up.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends entirely on device use. Most devices send one status message per day or fewer. When the button is pressed, recipients receive an alert message followed by location updates approximately once per minute for up to 30 minutes.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. A recipient may also ask the device owner to remove their number at any time.

Data retention

Alert and location records are retained only as long as needed to operate and troubleshoot the service, and are deleted when a device is retired or on request from the device owner.
