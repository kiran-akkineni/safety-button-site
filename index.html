Safety Button

A personal emergency alert device for families.

Safety Button is a small wearable button carried by a child or other family member. When the button is pressed and held, the device sends a text message and places an automated voice call to the phone numbers its owner has configured — typically the child's parents or guardians — including the device's location so they can respond.

The device connects over the cellular network and works anywhere with coverage. It has no screen and no other functions. It is operated by Kiran Akkineni and is currently in limited use by the operator's own family.

What the messaging program does

Program name: Safety Button Alerts

Recipients receive text messages from a Safety Button device in three situations:

  • Emergency alert. When the device owner presses and holds the button, every designated recipient receives a text message with the device's location, and an automated voice call.
  • Location updates. For up to 30 minutes after an alert, recipients receive a location update text approximately once per minute.
  • Device status. Occasional messages about the device itself, such as a low battery notice or a warning that the device has stopped checking in.

No marketing, promotional, or advertising messages are ever sent through this program.

How recipients consent to receive messages

Phone numbers are added to a device only by that device's owner, and only after the person who owns the number has given consent in person.

During device setup, the owner asks each prospective recipient — in person or by phone — whether they agree to receive emergency alert text messages from the device, and tells them the following:

"Safety Button will send you emergency alert and location text messages from this device. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. Our privacy policy and messaging terms are at kiran-akkineni.github.io/safety-button-site."

Only after the person agrees does the owner enter their mobile number into the device's configuration. Consent is active and affirmative: no number is ever pre-filled, pre-checked, or added by default.

There is no public sign-up form, no web-based sign-up, and no keyword sign-up campaign. Numbers are never purchased, rented, imported from lists, or obtained from any third party. Recipients are limited to the device owner's own family members and designated emergency contacts.

How recipients opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages immediately. Reply HELP for help. A recipient may also ask the device owner to remove their number from the device at any time, which takes effect immediately.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies and depends on device use. In normal operation a device sends one status message per day or fewer. When the button is pressed, recipients receive one alert message followed by location updates approximately once per minute for up to 30 minutes. Message and data rates may apply.
