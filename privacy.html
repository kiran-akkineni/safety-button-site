Privacy Policy

Safety Button · Operated by Kiran Akkineni · Last updated 26 July 2026

Safety Button is a personal emergency alert device. When its button is pressed, the device sends a text message and places an automated voice call to the phone numbers the device owner has configured. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Mobile information and messaging consent are never shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All categories of information described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Information sharing with subcontractors who provide support services, such as the telecommunications provider that transmits our messages, is permitted solely for the purpose of delivering the messages described in this policy. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never sold, rented, or licensed to anyone, for any purpose.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone numbers of the recipients the device owner designates, together with the record that each recipient consented to receive messages.
  • Device location — GPS coordinates, or an approximate location derived from nearby cell towers — captured when the button is pressed and during the alert period that follows.
  • Device status, such as battery level and periodic check-ins used to confirm the device is working.

We do not collect names, addresses, payment information, browsing activity, or any information about recipients beyond their mobile number.

How we use it

Information is used for one purpose: delivering alerts from a device to the recipients its owner has designated. Location is included in those alerts so recipients can find the person carrying the device. We do not use any of this information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics, and we do not send marketing messages of any kind.

Consent

Recipients are added by the device owner, and only after the recipient has given consent in person or by phone. Consent is active and affirmative — no number is pre-filled, pre-checked, or added by default. There is no public sign-up form and no numbers are ever purchased, rented, or obtained from third parties. A full description of the consent process is on our home page.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on device use. In normal operation a device sends one status message per day or fewer. When the button is pressed, recipients receive one alert message followed by location updates approximately once per minute for up to 30 minutes.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Safety Button does not charge recipients for messages; any charges from your mobile carrier are your responsibility.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. You will receive one confirmation message and nothing further. Reply HELP for help. A recipient may also ask the device owner to remove their number from the device at any time.

Data retention and security

Alert and location records are retained only as long as needed to operate and troubleshoot the service, and are deleted when a device is retired or on request from the device owner or a recipient. Data is held in access-controlled accounts with our cloud and telecommunications providers.

Children

A Safety Button device may be carried by a child, but the account and configuration are controlled entirely by that child's parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and the device collects no information about anyone other than its own location and status.

Your choices

You may request that we delete the information associated with your mobile number by emailing the address below. Opting out by replying STOP takes effect immediately and does not require contacting us.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available at this page, and the date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
